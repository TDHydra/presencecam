PresenceCam

A radar presence sensor with an OLED screen and a tamper-evident event log, for the ESP32 NodeMCU. Firmware version {{VERSION}}.

1What you need

2Wire it

FromTo ESP32 pin

The LD2410 needs 5 V on VCC; its signal pins are 3.3 V, so they connect straight to the ESP32.

3Install the firmware

Plug the board into this computer with USB, click the button and pick the port (it is usually called CP210x, USB Serial or CH340).

Your browser can't talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (not on a phone or in Safari or Firefox). This page has to be opened over https to use USB.

On a new board, let it erase the device when asked. It takes about a minute.

4Connect it to Wi-Fi

Right after the install, the flasher asks for your Wi-Fi name and password and sends them over the USB cable. That's it: the OLED shows the address, for example 192.168.1.42, and the board is also reachable at http://presencecam-xxxx.local.

Skipped that step? The board opens its own network called PresenceCam-XXXX. Join it with your phone; the setup page opens by itself (or go to http://192.168.4.1).

5Use it

  • OLED: shows PRESENT or clear, the distance and the radar signal strength. Press BOOT to cycle through the network and device pages.
  • Web page: live status, the event log with a Verified badge, settings (hold time, max distance, OLED type), Wi-Fi and firmware updates.
  • Blue LED on the board lights while someone is present.
  • Hold BOOT for 5 seconds to forget Wi-Fi and get the setup network back.
  • Logs & console: click the button above again with the board plugged in and choose Logs & console. Type help for commands.

Troubleshooting

No port shows up

Use a data cable; many USB cables only charge. Install the USB driver for your board's chip: CP210x or CH340. On Linux, add yourself to the dialout group.

"Failed to connect" or stuck at "Preparing"

Hold the BOOT button, click Connect & install again, and let go of BOOT once the progress bar moves. Close the Arduino IDE or any serial monitor that may have the port open.

The OLED stays dark or shows garbage

Check VCC to 3V3 and that SDA and SCL aren't swapped. Garbage, or a picture shifted by two pixels, usually means a 1.3" SH1106 screen: choose SH1106 on the web page, or type oled sh1106 in the console.

The radar says "OUT pin only" with an LD2410

TX and RX are crossed: LD2410 TX goes to D16, RX to D17. Swap them if needed. Presence still works from the OUT pin, without distance.

Presence stays on, or turns on with nobody there

Radar sees through thin walls and picks up fans and curtains. Lower the max distance on the web page, point the radar away from moving things, and adjust the hold time.

Brownout resets

The LD2410 draws current in bursts. Use a different USB port or a powered hub, or a short cable.
